Privacy Policy for Chainhop

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Chainhop (package com.chainhop.game) handles information. It applies to the app only.

Short version

Chainhop does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no in-app purchases. The app does not request permission to access the internet, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Information the app does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account of any kind.
  • No device identifiers, advertising ID, or IP address.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera access.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.

Information stored on your device

So the game can remember where you were, Chainhop saves the following in its own private storage area on your device:

  • Level progress, star ratings, and best scores.
  • Your selected colour theme and peg style, and which ones you have unlocked.
  • Your Chain Rush best runs, kept only on this device.
  • Your current life count and the time the last one was used, so lives refill correctly.

This data never leaves your device. It is not readable by other apps, and Android deletes it when you uninstall the game. You can clear it at any time through Android's Settings → Apps → Chainhop → Storage → Clear data.

Permissions

Chainhop requests a single Android permission, VIBRATE, used for haptic feedback when pegs are moved. It requests no internet permission.

Children

Chainhop collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It contains no advertising, no purchases, and no links out to other services.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Chainhop adds advertising, in-app purchases, or online leaderboards, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. Continuing to use the app after an update means the revised policy applies.
